## Source and scope

- Source type: Peer-reviewed PV module-temperature model
- Site, cultivar, equipment, and study scale: Seven silicon modules on an
  open rack in the Negev desert; module tilt reported as 30.9° in pvlib docs
- What was measured or derived: Empirical relation for module temperature
  from POA irradiance, ambient temperature, and wind.
- Relevant page, table, figure, or equation, or dataset fields: pvlib
  `temperature.faiman` API identifies the paper and documents default `u0=25`,
  `u1=6.84`; original article equation/table page not independently inspected.
