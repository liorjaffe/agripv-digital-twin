## Decision log

- Decision: Retain the values as explicitly attributed, unverified model
  parameters; do not describe them as reconciled with Van Henten (1994).
- Rationale: The primary full text and equation/table locations were not
  inspected in this update.
- Date: 2026-10-13
- Follow-up needed:
  - Identify and document the 2023 secondary source that reportedly informed
    some defaults; retain it as a separate source record if recovered.
  - Obtain the publisher-authorized paper and verify `c_gamma` and `c_q10_gr`
    against the growth-rate equation/table.
  - Verify `c_resp_sht`, `c_resp_rt`, `c_q10_resp` and their 25 °C reference
    against the respiration equations/table.
  - Verify `c_omega`, `c_ggamma`, `c_q10_ggamma`, `c_epsilon`, `g_bnd`, and
    `g_stm` against the photosynthesis/conductance equations/table, including
    units and reference temperature.
  - Verify all three `c_car` coefficients and the 5–40 °C domain against the
    paper; retain the clamp as a project adaptation if the article does not
    specify that validity range.
  - 2026-10-13 (TEMP-001, CROP-003): Van Henten's own sensitivity analysis
    (PhD thesis, 1994, https://doi.org/10.18174/205106) finds temperature
    "relatively less important" in this model. In this project the structure
    produced higher daily dry-mass gain in cool air (known limitation,
    CROP-003). TEMP-001 is an exploratory option, off by default. Revisit when
    coefficients are verified.
